9. Test substitution approval.
10. Open Shopper and Driver operations and complete required safety checks.
11. Test pharmacy chain-of-custody simulation.
12. Open professional coach marketplace and submit a synthetic appointment request.
13. Open Master Admin analytics and confirm that private subscriber content is not shown.

## Privacy boundary

Master Admin may view operational status, subscriptions, service availability, retailer readiness, staff roles, feature flags and privacy-safe analytics. Master Admin must not receive routine access to subscriber health details, height, weight, goals, pantry contents, photos, progress, meal plans, supplement logs or private AI coaching conversations.

## Simulation limitations

This Alpha does not process real payments, place retailer orders, dispense or transport prescriptions, book real professional appointments, use live grocery prices, or provide medical care. All marketplace, pharmacy and AI-provider outputs are synthetic test flows unless clearly labelled otherwise.

## Device installation

Open the hosted HTTPS address in Chrome or Safari. Use the browser menu and select **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen** when offered.
