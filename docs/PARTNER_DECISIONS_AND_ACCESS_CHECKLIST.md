# Project 220 — Partner Decisions and Access Checklist

This checklist contains only items that require owner, partner, legal, financial, retailer, or account-holder action.

## Business identity

- Final corporation and marketplace legal name
- Operating name for Project 220
- Business address and service address
- Support email and telephone number
- Privacy contact
- Copyright and trademark owner
- Initial launch territory and service zones

## Grocery and supplement marketplace

- Approve the launch merchant list: No Frills, Giant Tiger, Walmart, Real Canadian Superstore, Safeway, Sobeys, FreshCo, Save-On-Foods, Dollarama, Dollar Tree, GNC, and Popeye's Supplements
- Decide which merchants are included in the first Winnipeg pilot
- Obtain permissions, feeds, affiliate arrangements, APIs, or marketplace agreements where available
- Approve the fallback manual shopper-purchase model for merchants without integrations
- Set markup rules by merchant, category, and product
- Set service fee, delivery fee, minimum order, cancellation fee, and refund rules
- Set the maximum payment authorization tolerance for final price differences
- Decide shopper, driver, and shopper-driver compensation
- Confirm the policy that tips are passed through to the intended worker
- Approve service hours and delivery radius

## Healthy restaurant alternatives

- Define what qualifies as a healthy restaurant alternative
- Select the first restaurant partners or data provider
- Decide whether restaurant orders use direct integrations, approved feeds, checkout handoff, or Project 220 fulfillment
- Approve ranking modes: healthiest fit, lowest price, fastest delivery, preferred restaurant, and best overall
- Approve nutrition and allergen disclaimers for restaurant data
- Set restaurant markup and delivery policies

## Pantry AI and household privacy

- Approve default photo setting shown at onboarding while preserving user choice to store or delete after processing
- Set stored-photo retention and deletion periods
- Approve receipt and pantry-image privacy language
- Select barcode and product-data providers
- Select image, receipt, and OCR providers
- Approve expiry reminder timing and default notification schedule
- Approve child-profile and household-member privacy rules

## Premium cookbook

- Set premium price and trial terms
- Confirm in-app viewing only with no PDF download, publishing, selling, public links, or external export at launch
- Approve dietary, allergy, family-size, and bulk-cooking options
- Approve recipe and nutrition disclaimers

## AI and professional coaching

- Define Free, Premium, and Professional subscription tiers
- Set AI coaching limits and reminder defaults
- Define permitted professional categories and credential requirements
- Confirm dietitians and support workers are excluded from the initial professional marketplace
- Set professional commission, booking, cancellation, refund, and payout rules
- Approve emergency, medical, wellness, exercise, nutrition, and supplement disclaimer language
- Select credential-verification and background-check providers if required

## Payments and banking

- Open or confirm the corporation bank account
- Select the payment processor and create production and sandbox accounts
- Complete processor identity and business verification
- Approve tax, deposit, refund, chargeback, and payout handling
- Decide whether payment is authorized at checkout and captured after fulfillment
- Do not place passwords, keys, certificates, banking credentials, or signing secrets in chat or source code

## Legal and insurance

- Obtain legal review for Canadian privacy, consumer protection, marketplace terms, food delivery, subscriptions, refunds, health claims, accessibility, taxes, and worker classification
- Prepare customer, household, shopper, driver, professional coach, merchant, and restaurant terms
- Prepare privacy policy, consent policy, data retention policy, cookie policy, and acceptable-use policy
- Confirm insurance requirements: commercial general liability, cyber, errors and omissions, delivery/vehicle, and any marketplace coverage
- Confirm food-handling and delivery requirements in the launch jurisdiction

## Accounts and infrastructure

- Domain and DNS account
- Business email service
- GitHub organization decision
- Production hosting account
- Managed PostgreSQL database
- Private object storage
- Email and push notification providers
- Error monitoring and analytics accounts
- Google Play developer account
- Apple Developer account
- Payment provider accounts

## Branding and stores

- Final logo, app icon, splash screen, colours, and typography
- App Store and Google Play publisher names
- Store descriptions, screenshots, support page, and privacy page
- Final subscription names and pricing

## Pilot operations

- Recruit test households
- Recruit and screen initial shoppers and drivers
- Select pilot merchants and healthy restaurant alternatives
- Define support hours and escalation contacts
- Run simulated orders before accepting real payments
- Approve pilot launch only after payment, refund, privacy, safety, and delivery tests pass
