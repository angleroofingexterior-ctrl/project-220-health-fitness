# Project 220 — Origin and Brand Foundation

## Founder origin

Project 220 began as Marcel Joseph Goulet's personal system for pursuing a lifelong goal: reaching 220 pounds through structured training, nutrition, recovery and discipline.

The project expanded when Marcel recognized that the same type of organized support could help other people pursue their own goals. Project 220 is therefore not limited to weight gain or one body type. It is intended to help people build healthier routines around their own starting point, abilities, goals, culture, budget and lifestyle.

## Core story

> Project 220 was born from one personal goal and grew into a platform for everyone. It helps people turn a dream into a practical plan through fitness, nutrition, progress tracking, food planning and supportive daily action.

## Mission

Help people pursue meaningful physical-fitness and healthy-lifestyle goals with practical, personalized and accessible tools.

## Vision

Build a trusted global health-and-fitness platform that connects training, nutrition, pantry planning, grocery support, coaching and progress tracking without losing sight of the individual person behind every goal.

## Brand promise

**Your goal. Your plan. Your progress.**

Project 220 should never imply that every person needs to weigh 220 pounds. The name represents the founder's original goal and the principle that every user deserves a system built around their own goal.

## Product principles

1. Personal goals, not one universal body standard.
2. Health and sustainability before speed or appearance.
3. Clear user control over plans, data and privacy.
4. Inclusive support for different bodies, abilities, cultures and budgets.
5. Honest limitations and no invented medical certainty.
6. Food-allergy and dietary restrictions treated as hard safety constraints.
7. Human confirmation before acting on AI-detected pantry items.
8. Transparent subscription, delivery and tipping costs.
9. Optional tips with 100% allocated to the assigned shopper or driver.
10. Build locally in Winnipeg and Manitoba, then expand responsibly.

## Corporate relationship

Project 220 will operate as a distinct product and brand owned by the founders' future corporation. Other applications may also be owned by that corporation, but Project 220 must retain separate branding, accounts, product records, finances and legal documentation where practical.

The final corporation name, ownership details and legal statements must be inserted only after the founders complete registration and obtain professional advice.

## Initial audience

- People gaining healthy body weight or muscle
- People losing weight
- Beginners who need structured guidance
- Experienced users who want planning and tracking
- People managing food budgets
- Users who benefit from pantry-aware meal planning
- Customers who need grocery shopping or delivery assistance
- Coaches and community programs in later releases
