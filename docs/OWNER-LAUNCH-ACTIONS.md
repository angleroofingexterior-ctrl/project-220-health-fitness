# Project 220 Owner Launch Actions

These actions require the legal owner or an authorized company representative. Development cannot safely move into live payments or public delivery until the applicable items are complete.

## Immediate decisions

- [ ] Confirm final legal business name.
- [ ] Confirm ownership percentages and sign the founders/shareholders agreement.
- [ ] Choose whether the company will be incorporated provincially or federally.
- [ ] Approve Winnipeg as the closed pilot zone before Manitoba-wide expansion.
- [ ] Approve the proposed subscription and delivery pricing for pilot testing.

## Business and banking

- [ ] Register the business.
- [ ] Obtain a CRA business number.
- [ ] Open a business bank account.
- [ ] Set up bookkeeping and sales-tax advice.
- [ ] Create a business support email and phone number.

## Legal and privacy

- [ ] Retain a Manitoba lawyer.
- [ ] Review privacy policy, terms, health disclaimer, delivery terms and driver agreement.
- [ ] Confirm driver classification and payment rules.
- [ ] Confirm customer cancellation, refund and substitution policies.
- [ ] Confirm consent and deletion rules for pantry, fridge, receipt and delivery photos.
- [ ] Approve the nondiscrimination and accessibility policies.

## Insurance

- [ ] Obtain commercial general liability coverage.
- [ ] Obtain cyber/privacy coverage.
- [ ] Obtain errors and omissions coverage.
- [ ] Obtain non-owned automobile and delivery/courier coverage.
- [ ] Define proof-of-insurance requirements for drivers.

## Required production accounts

- [ ] Create a Canadian-region Supabase project or approved equivalent.
- [ ] Create Stripe and Stripe Connect business accounts.
- [ ] Create Apple Developer and Google Play Console accounts.
- [ ] Create a maps/routing provider account.
- [ ] Create email, push-notification and customer-support accounts.
- [ ] Create an approved AI image-recognition account.
- [ ] Create crash reporting and monitoring accounts.
- [ ] Purchase or connect the Project 220 domain.

## Retail and delivery pilot

- [ ] Contact Walmart Canada, Loblaw/Superstore, No Frills and other target retailers about official integration or permitted shopping workflows.
- [ ] Recruit 5–15 pilot drivers.
- [ ] Define identity, licence, insurance and background-screening requirements.
- [ ] Recruit 25–100 invited Winnipeg households.
- [ ] Establish pilot support hours and an emergency escalation process.
- [ ] Decide driver base compensation, mileage reimbursement and wait-time policy.

## Secrets and access

- [ ] Never place live API keys in GitHub, screenshots, email threads or chat messages.
- [ ] Add secrets only to the selected hosting platform's encrypted secret manager.
- [ ] Enable MFA on GitHub, banking, payment, hosting and developer-store accounts.
- [ ] Limit administrator access to the minimum number of people.

## Launch authorization gates

The owner must not authorize public launch until all of the following are signed off:

- [ ] Legal review complete.
- [ ] Insurance active.
- [ ] Production payment account approved.
- [ ] Driver payout testing complete.
- [ ] Security and privacy testing complete.
- [ ] Refund and cancellation testing complete.
- [ ] Customer support process staffed.
- [ ] Closed Winnipeg pilot completed successfully.
- [ ] Manitoba expansion economics reviewed and approved.
