# Project 220 — Partner Decisions and External Requirements

This list contains the items the owners must decide, purchase, authorize, or confirm before the related production features can be activated.

## Business identity

- final corporation and operating names
- registered business address
- support email and phone number
- privacy contact
- copyright and trademark ownership
- initial marketplace launch area

## Marketplace pricing

- grocery and supplement markup method
- restaurant markup or commission method
- delivery fees
- service fees
- minimum order
- multi-store surcharge
- cancellation and failed-delivery fees
- shopper compensation
- driver compensation
- refund and price-adjustment policy
- whether shoppers may also serve as drivers
- confirmation that workers retain 100% of tips

## Subscription pricing

Approve prices and included features for:

- Individual
- Two-Person Household
- Small Family
- Large Family
- Extended Household
- Custom or Assisted Shopping Plan

Confirm how pricing changes with active account users, number of people shopped for, shopping frequency, number of stores, life-stage mix, premium cookbook access, AI features, and coaching tier.

## Retailer and restaurant operations

- prioritize live integrations versus manual shopping for each retailer
- approve launch retailers and service areas
- select initial healthy restaurant partners or directory sources
- decide whether loyalty accounts and points may be used
- approve pricing-refresh frequency and stale-price messaging
- approve substitution response deadline

## Food safety and delivery

Obtain local professional review of the shopper and driver safe-food-handling policy, including:

- required training or certification
- hand hygiene and glove rules
- cross-contamination prevention
- raw meat and ready-to-eat food separation
- produce bagging and tamper-evident closure rules
- bag labelling, weight, quantity, and timestamp requirements
- temperature-control requirements
- vehicle cleanliness
- photo-evidence and retention policy
- incident reporting and worker suspension rules

## Pharmacy pickup

Before pharmacy pickup can be activated, obtain legal and operational approval for:

- customer authorization language
- pharmacy agreements
- privacy and health-information handling
- identity and signature verification
- restricted medication categories
- sealed-package requirements
- cold-chain or special handling
- failed-delivery return procedures
- insurance coverage
- payment handling for prescription cost, co-pay, and service fees

No pharmacy feature should be publicly activated until these approvals are documented.

## Coaching marketplace

- approved professional categories
- credential verification standard
- background-check policy
- platform commission
- coach subscription and customer pricing
- cancellation and dispute policies
- professional liability insurance requirements
- emergency escalation language

Dietitians and support workers are excluded from the initial professional marketplace according to the current product decision.

## Technology accounts

- production domain and DNS access
- business email service
- cloud database
- authentication provider
- private file and photo storage
- transaction email and notification service
- payment processor
- maps, routing, barcode, nutrition, and product-data providers
- error monitoring and analytics
- Google Play developer account
- Apple Developer account

## Security credentials

The owners must create and retain:

- Android signing key
- Apple signing credentials
- payment and cloud secrets
- production environment variables
- backup recovery credentials

Do not commit any credentials to GitHub or paste them into ordinary chat messages.

## Legal documents

Professional review and approval are needed for:

- customer terms
- shopper terms
- driver terms
- coach marketplace terms
- privacy policy
- consent and photo-retention policy
- medical and wellness disclaimer
- pharmacy pickup terms
- food-safety policy
- refund and cancellation policy
- subscription terms
- data retention and deletion policy
- accessibility statement

## Insurance and compliance

Confirm required coverage for:

- general commercial liability
- cyber and privacy incidents
- marketplace operations
- food delivery and contamination claims
- vehicle and courier operations
- professional coaching
- pharmacy pickup and chain of custody
- workers, contractors, and workplace requirements

## Branding and store publication

- final logo and app icon
- brand colours and typography
- splash screen
- official screenshots
- app-store description
- support website
- privacy-policy URL
- age rating and content declarations
- final subscription names and prices

## Beta launch resources

- customer beta testers
- shopper testers
- driver testers
- participating stores or manual-shopping test locations
- healthy restaurant test partners
- test households representing different life stages and accessibility-related diet needs
- test Android and iOS devices
- customer-support process during the pilot
